Slime Studies

A simulated Physarum slime mould (Jeff Jones's agent model) put to work. In each study something places food, the slime grows toward it, and wherever it gathers is the answer.

Every decision in a Pokémon battle. Ten food sites ring the slime, one per move or switch, and the option it gathers on is played. One slime is fed by a PPO network; the other was evolved with CMA-ES to play on its own senses, and beats the network (71% against the strongest-move bot).
