A Smaller Artin Group with No Geometric CAT(0) Action

Geometric group theory · Lean · AI-assisted

An edit of OpenAI's preprint that refutes the CAT(0) conjecture for Artin groups. A shorter bound on braid translation lengths cuts their example from 116 generators to 29, with the full proof checked in Lean. A further version gets it to 11 generators, checked except for one standard assumption (van der Lek's theorem on parabolic subgroups). This was almost entirely AI-driven. I'm listing it as an experiment in that workflow, not as my own math.
