Privacy

What this site and Lilypad do with cookies, ads, and files. Updated nov 2026.

This site

frogscooper.dev

frogscooper.dev is a static site. It does not set cookies of its own, and it does not run analytics. Pages do load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser requests those files from Google.

Every page includes a Google AdSense account tag (ca-pub-9008261213236010) so Google can verify the publisher. This site does not load an ad script, and it does not show ads.

Lilypad ads

lilypad.frogscooper.dev

lilypad.frogscooper.dev shows Google AdSense ads. Those ads use cookies. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Lilypad asks for consent before using them.

You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. Lilypad also has its own privacy page.

Files

Image, PDF, and other files you open in Lilypad stay on your device. Conversion and the other tools run in the browser. Files are never uploaded.
